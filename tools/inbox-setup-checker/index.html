Free tool

Free inbox setup checker

Enter a domain to check SPF, DKIM, DMARC and MX. MX points to the servers that receive mail. SPF names who may send it. DKIM publishes the key that signs each message. DMARC tells receivers what to do when those checks fail. p=none only monitors and sends reports. Quarantine and reject take action. The lookup runs in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent to Be Leaded.

Check a domain

Protocol, path, and a leading www are ignored. https://www.example.com/path checks example.com.
One label, such as google or selector1. Leave this blank to skip DKIM.

Each lookup goes to Google Public DNS and times out after 5 seconds. This page does not save the domain, and it does not send it to Be Leaded.
