    Most outbound fails before anyone reads it.

    It breaks in one of three places. We fix all three before your first email goes out.

    StageWhat usually happensWhat we do

    The list

    What usually happens

    Scraped contacts, stale job titles and catch-all domains. Emails bounce, and the ones that land reach the wrong person.

    What we do

    Current, verified contacts matched to your buyer. Anything we can't verify stays off the list.

    The inbox

    What usually happens

    A new domain, no warmup and half-finished DNS. The email goes to spam and nobody finds out.

    What we do

    Separate sending domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, two to three weeks of warmup, and blocklist checks every morning.

    The email

    What usually happens

    A template with a first-name field. Prospects spot it in one line and delete it.

    What we do

    Short plain-text emails built on something true about each account, written the way people write to each other.

    Frequently asked questions

    If yours isn't here, ask it on the call.

    What does Be Leaded do?

    Be Leaded is a B2B marketing agency. We run cold email and appointment setting, LinkedIn outreach, paid social and search, newsletter, email and SMS programs, AI voice follow-up, and off-market deal flow for M&A advisors and business brokers. We build the infrastructure, write every message and book meetings on your calendar.

    How soon will we see meetings?

    Most programs go live in about three weeks, once domains are warmed and the first sequences are approved. Replies start soon after launch. How many turn into meetings depends on your market and your offer, and we'll give you a realistic range on the first call.

    Will cold email hurt our company domain?

    No. We send from separate domains that we set up, warm and monitor every day. Your company domain never carries cold volume.

    Who writes the emails?

    We do. Every sequence is written in plain text for your market, and nothing sends until you approve it.

    What does it cost?

    Programs start at $2,000 a month. For the right fit we also price per qualified meeting. You'll get an exact number after a 30-minute call.

    Are your emails compliant?

    Every email identifies the sender, includes a physical mailing address and a clear way to opt out, and opt-outs are honored right away. We follow CAN-SPAM on every US send and check local rules before sending into the EU, UK or Canada.

    Tell us who you want to meet.

    Thirty minutes with the founder. You'll leave with a clear picture of your market, what a campaign would look like and what it would cost.
