How it works

Three steps, one introduction, no fee to you.

  1. Day 1

    Tell us what you need

    Send the request above or book a call. We ask a few questions about your buyer, budget and what a good outcome looks like.

  2. Day 1 to 2

    We check fit

  3. Within a business day

Questions about Match

What is Be Leaded Match?

A free matching service for companies that need an agency, a tool, a consultant or an advisor. You tell us what you need, we check fit against partners whose references and results we've verified, and we make one warm introduction. Partners pay a fee per qualified introduction, which we disclose.

Is it really free?

Yes, for the company asking. Partners pay us per qualified introduction, and there are no listing fees, so nobody pays to be recommended.

What if Be Leaded could do the work?

We'll say so and lay out both options. Some needs are ours to run, such as cold email and newsletters. Many aren't, such as outbound in a language we don't write, a CRM implementation or a capital raise, and those go to partners.

How do you vet partners?

References we speak to, results we check, and a clear specialty. We also watch how they respond to introductions, and partners who go quiet are removed.

Can you help an owner raise capital or sell?

Yes. We introduce owners to capital partners and M&A advisors we've checked, and the partner relationship is disclosed. We don't advise on the transaction ourselves.

How do partners join?

Email us with your specialty, two references and examples of results. If it's a fit, we agree a per-introduction fee and add you to the network.

Tell us who you want to meet.

Thirty minutes with the founder. You'll leave with a clear picture of your market, what a campaign would look like and what it would cost.
