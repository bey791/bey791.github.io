Off-market deal flow for M&A advisors and business brokers

Be Leaded finds owners of privately held companies who may be open to a sale, a recapitalization or a valuation, and starts confidential conversations in your firm's name. You get meetings with owners who want to talk. We don't advise on transactions. Your team does.

The best deals are rarely listed.

Many owners of solid businesses never put them on the market. When they do sell, it's often to the advisor or buyer who reached them first and treated the conversation with care. That's the conversation we start for you.

How we approach owners

Short, specific and respectful.

Owners of good businesses are approached constantly. The ones who reply are answering a person who clearly knows their business and isn't in a hurry.

  • A short note from a real person at your firm
  • Curiosity about the business, with no claim that a buyer is waiting
  • An offer of a confidential valuation or a short conversation
  • Easy to decline, and opt-outs honored right away

Clear lines

We start conversations. Your team runs the deal.

Our job ends when an owner is on your calendar. Everything after that belongs to your advisors.

  • We don't advise on transactions, value businesses or negotiate terms

Questions about deal flow

Ask anything else on the call.

What is off-market deal sourcing?

Off-market deal sourcing means finding companies that aren't listed for sale and reaching their owners directly. Many privately held businesses are never formally marketed, so an advisor or buyer who starts the conversation early can talk to the owner before a formal process begins.

How do you find owners who might sell?

We research companies that match your mandate by industry, geography, revenue band, tenure and ownership, then prioritize owners showing signals such as no named successor, a first general manager hire or a founder who still runs sales. Every contact is verified before outreach.

Do you reach out in our firm's name?

Yes. We set up sending domains tied to your brand and write in your voice. You approve every email before it sends, and meetings are booked directly with your advisors.

Is the outreach confidential?

Yes. We never name a client or a buyer without your approval, we never publish owner names, and owner data stays private to your engagement.

Which industries do you cover?

Any industry with privately held companies in your size range. Common mandates include home and commercial services, manufacturing, distribution, healthcare services and business services.

Do you work with buyers as well as advisors?

Yes. Independent sponsors, search funds, family offices and platform companies use the same process to source add-on acquisitions directly from owners.

What does it cost?

Programs start at $2,000 a month. The exact number depends on the size of your mandate and the channels we run, and you'll have it after a 30-minute call.

Tell us who you want to meet.

Thirty minutes with the founder. You'll leave with a clear picture of your market, what a campaign would look like and what it would cost.
