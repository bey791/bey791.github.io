Belkins: best for guaranteed appointment volume at mid-market+ budgets

Belkins is best for teams that want steady meeting volume, an appointment-setting-first story, and a vendor with broad brand recognition in mid-market and enterprise searches. If your ACV and pipeline plan can support a mid-to-high five-figure monthly program, Belkins is often on the shortlist for a reason.

Typical pricing band (third-party): often listed around $5,000–$15,000 a month. Reported ranges vary; confirm with Belkins.

Strengths: review volume, packaged meetings, clear appointment-setting positioning.

Tradeoffs: can feel heavy for SMB budgets or early offer validation; less “from $2,000” transparency than smaller DFY shops. Packaged meeting commitments also assume your offer and ICP are stable enough to absorb volume. If you are still validating messaging, a lighter program may teach you faster before you buy Belkins-scale capacity.

Fair close: if you need Belkins-class volume and you have the budget, talk to them—this page is not an argument against that fit.

For a deeper read on what retainers usually include, see our guide to cold email agency pricing.

Cleverly: best for LinkedIn-first outreach on a tight budget

Cleverly productizes LinkedIn outreach with published low entry tiers—often cited from about $397 a month for LinkedIn packages. That makes it a common Belkins vs Cleverly comparison when budget and channel are the main variables.

Strengths: transparent tiers, LinkedIn specialization, accessible for smaller teams testing outbound.

Tradeoffs: single-channel limits if your buyers live in email or if you need enterprise multi-touch cycles; LinkedIn volume caps are real. Pick Cleverly when a LinkedIn-only test is the job. Pick a multi-channel DFY shop when email deliverability, reply handling, and calendar booking matter as much as connection requests.

If LinkedIn is part of a broader mix, compare with a dedicated LinkedIn lead generation program that times outreach with email.

CIENCE: best for enterprise-scale SDR + data platform buyers

CIENCE sells a tech-enabled multichannel / SDR-as-a-service motion with a strong data-platform narrative. Belkins vs CIENCE usually comes down to packaging: appointment packages versus platform-plus-services scale.

Pricing: platform plus services; full-service engagements often land in the mid-to-high thousands per month on third-party bands. Confirm on a call.

Strengths: scale, data ops, enterprise reporting.

Tradeoffs: overkill for lean teams; more platform feel than boutique relationship. If you need an SDR army and a data stack under one roof, CIENCE belongs on the list. If you want a lean, personalized program without enterprise packaging theater, look elsewhere on this page.

Martal Group: best for full-funnel / fractional sales partnership (esp. B2B tech)

Martal positions as a fractional sales / multi-channel partner—email, LinkedIn, phone, and a sales-team-extension model that fits B2B tech and SaaS motions.

Strengths: outbound plus process partnership; useful when you need more than calendar fills.

Tradeoffs: retainers often higher (directories commonly cite roughly $4,000–$10,000+); heavier engagement than “just book meetings.” Choose Martal when you want a fractional sales org. Choose a meeting-focused agency when the bottleneck is simply getting qualified conversations on the calendar.

Leadium: best for flexible / month-to-month outbound programs

Leadium is often cited as a boutique-style DFY outbound shop with flexibility and month-to-month options as a differentiator versus annual meeting packages.

Pricing: low-to-mid thousands typical in directories. Confirm current packaging on a call.

Strengths: accountability via flexible contracts; emphasis on data validation.

Tradeoffs: not the “guaranteed yearly meetings” Belkins story. If contract flexibility is your top filter among Belkins competitors, Leadium earns a slot. Still press on meeting definition, domains, and reply SLA before you sign.

When to look past Belkins (decision guide)

Use this as a routing sheet, not a verdict. The right Belkins alternative is the one whose channels, meeting definition, and contract match the job you need done in the next 90 days.

  • Budget under ~$5k/mo but you still need real DFY email + LinkedIn → Be Leaded, Leadium, or Cleverly (channel-dependent)
  • LinkedIn-only cheap test → Cleverly
  • Enterprise data platform + SDR army → CIENCE
  • Fractional sales org, not just meetings → Martal
  • Guaranteed high meeting packages and you’ll pay for brand → Belkins

Five diligence questions before you sign any Belkins alternative

  1. How do you define a qualified meeting (and who owns no-shows)?
  2. Whose domains send—and what happens to my brand domain?
  3. Who writes copy, and do I approve before send?
  4. What’s the reply SLA, and who books the calendar?
  5. What’s included at the quoted price (data, infra, LinkedIn, reporting)?

FAQ

What are the best Belkins alternatives in 2026?

It depends on fit. Cleverly suits LinkedIn-first budgets; CIENCE suits enterprise SDR plus data platform buyers; Martal suits full-funnel fractional sales; Leadium suits flexible month-to-month outbound; Be Leaded suits high-personalization email and LinkedIn with appointment setting from about $2,000 a month. Belkins remains strong when you want packaged mid-market appointment volume and will pay for it.

How does Belkins pricing compare to other cold email agencies?

Third-party listicles often place Belkins around $5,000–$15,000 a month. Productized LinkedIn options can start much lower; many done-for-you email agencies sit in the low-to-mid thousands. Be Leaded programs start at about $2,000 a month. Always confirm current packages on a sales call.

When should I choose Be Leaded instead of Belkins?

Choose Be Leaded when you want account-level personalization, separate-domain infrastructure, LinkedIn coordinated with email, human same-day reply handling, and meetings booked on your calendar—without an enterprise-only price floor. Choose Belkins when you specifically want their packaged appointment-volume model and mid-market or enterprise brand fit.

Are Cleverly, CIENCE, Martal, and Leadium good Belkins competitors?

Yes, for different jobs. Cleverly competes on LinkedIn price and simplicity; CIENCE on enterprise scale and data; Martal on full-funnel sales partnership; Leadium on flexible done-for-you outbound. Compare channels, meeting definitions, and contract terms—not logos.

Will switching from Belkins (or another agency) hurt my domain reputation?

It shouldn’t if the new partner sends from separate, warmed domains with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC—and never puts cold volume on your primary company domain. Ask any Belkins alternative how domains are set up before you migrate. Be Leaded builds and monitors separate sending domains for each program.
