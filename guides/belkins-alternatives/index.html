Why people look for Belkins alternatives

Most buyers are not dumping Belkins because the work is bad. They are checking fit before they sign, or they need a second quote. The common reasons, mirrored across top-ranking comparison pages in 2026:

  • Price floor. When we checked on October 18, 2026, Belkins' appointment setting page listed a starter averaging $5,000. The pricing page from that read names four plans and prints no dollar prices. Growth is one name. Growth Plus is the next. For small business is a separate heading. Enterprise is the last. Third-party Clutch data commonly places typical projects in the $10,000 to $49,999 band. Under that mid-market floor, buyers look elsewhere.
  • Contract wording on the pricing page. Belkins calls the plans monthly retainer packages. The Enterprise block says contract terms are custom (belkins.io/pricing).
  • Who delivers For small business. That same pricing page says partnering agencies in the Belkins ecosystem deliver it.
  • Channel mix. The glance table lists the channels each firm publishes. Read that row against the channel your buyers actually answer.

If Belkins matches your budget, channel mix, and contract appetite, stay with that shortlist. This page exists for everyone else.

CIENCE: best for published SDR pricing plus a data platform

CIENCE sells SDR-as-a-service paired with its graph8 data and AI stack. Belkins vs CIENCE usually comes down to packaging: appointment packages versus platform-plus-services with itemized public pricing.

Public pricing (cience.com/pricing, checked October 2026): $5,000 one-time GTM system setup; $2,000 a month strategic execution; $499 a month graph8 platform license; optional SDR seats published from $1,500 (offshore Level 1) upward by region.

Strengths: rare price transparency at this tier, month-to-month blocks, scale, data ops.

Tradeoffs: the public menu is a platform license plus optional seats, which is a different invoice from a single retainer. Read the pod named on the order form before you sign.

Cleverly: best for LinkedIn-first outreach on a tight budget

Cleverly productizes LinkedIn outreach and is the usual Belkins vs Cleverly comparison when budget and channel are the main variables. Its own pricing page returned 404 when we checked in October 2026, so treat current numbers as quote-led. Clutch still shows a most-common project size under $10,000 on recent third-party audits.

A dated vendor read of these published plans is the guide Cleverly alternatives.

Strengths: accessible for smaller teams testing outbound; LinkedIn specialization; historically transparent tiers.

Tradeoffs: single-channel limits if your buyers live in email or need multi-touch enterprise cycles. Pick Cleverly when a LinkedIn-only test is the job. Pick a multi-channel DFY shop when email deliverability, reply handling, and calendar booking matter as much as connection requests.

If LinkedIn is part of a broader mix, compare with a dedicated LinkedIn lead generation program that times outreach with email.

Martal Group: best for full-funnel / fractional sales partnership

Martal positions as a fractional sales and multi-channel partner: email, LinkedIn, phone, and a sales-team-extension model that fits B2B tech and SaaS motions. Own-site pricing is quote-only. Clutch common project size sits near Belkins ($10,000 to $49,000).

Strengths: senior onshore seller narrative; outbound plus process partnership; useful when you need more than calendar fills.

Tradeoffs: heavier engagement than just book meetings; multi-month pilots are common. Choose Martal when you want a fractional sales org. Choose a meeting-focused agency when the bottleneck is simply getting qualified conversations on the calendar.

Belkins: baseline for packaged mid-market appointment volume

Belkins is the baseline on this page for teams that want an appointment-setting package. Founded in 2017, it describes done-for-you outbound across email, LinkedIn, and calling.

Belkins pricing

Opened October 18, 2026 on Belkins' own pages. The appointment URL prints a starter average of $5,000. The same screen names three channels (belkins.io/appointment-setting). The pricing URL leaves the dollar field blank. It names four plans and prints no dollar prices (belkins.io/pricing):

  • For small business. Cold email and LinkedIn. Calling is not included. Belkins says partner agencies in its ecosystem deliver this package.
  • Growth. Cold email, LinkedIn, intent calling, and a messenger seat. ABM and ads stay off this tier.
  • Growth Plus. Folds cold calling plus ABM and ads into the Growth channel list.
  • Enterprise. Custom strategy, custom contract terms, and add-ons on demand. No public price.

Every package on that page includes a sales audit, buyer profile work, manual lead research, copy, scheduling, and reporting. Ask Belkins for the live quote, then set it beside one retainer.

Five diligence questions before you sign any Belkins alternative

  1. How do you define a qualified meeting, and what happens when a prospect cancels?
  2. Whose domains send, and what happens to my brand domain?
  3. Who writes copy, and do I approve before send?
  4. What is the reply SLA, and who books the calendar?
  5. What is included at the quoted price (data, infra, LinkedIn, reporting)?

Are CIENCE, Cleverly, Martal, SalesRoads, Callbox, and Leadium good Belkins competitors?
