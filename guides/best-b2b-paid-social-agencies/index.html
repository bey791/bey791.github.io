Best B2B paid social agencies for pipeline, not just leads

By Ahmad Khan, Founder. Last reviewed November 12, 2026.

B2B paid social agencies plan and run paid ads, usually on LinkedIn and Meta, to reach business buyers and create pipeline, not just cheap leads. Some firms specialize in LinkedIn only, some run LinkedIn with Meta, Google or other channels and some attach creative, landing pages or follow-up to the ads. Be Leaded is one option on this list, running Meta, Google and LinkedIn campaigns with a landing page and a reply by email, SMS or voice; compare models below.

When to hire a B2B paid social agency

Hire help when the job is bigger than the hours you can put on LinkedIn Campaign Manager or Meta Ads Manager each week. That usually means the brief has to cover more than one offer, the creative has to be tested rather than left running, and reporting has to follow pipeline or booked meetings rather than clicks alone. An agency is also a fit when you want a named model from this list: a LinkedIn-only firm, a multi-channel paid social team or a program that attaches landing pages and a reply to each lead.

Keep the work in house when someone on your team already runs the channels well, owns the ad accounts and can turn a lead into a conversation without a delay you cannot live with. A review or a one-time audit can still be useful in that case. It should not be treated as a substitute for a hired program if no one inside the company will do the weekly work.

On a first call, ask who will do the weekly work, what the firm counts as a qualified lead or a qualified conversation, who takes the meeting after a form is submitted and how reporting will follow pipeline rather than clicks alone. If those answers are vague, the model on this list may look right while the delivery is not.

This page does not tell you that hiring always beats an internal team, and it does not promise a result in a set window. It is a way to shortlist firms whose own sites describe the job you actually need.

LinkedIn and Meta in the same program

LinkedIn and Meta often do different work in a B2B plan. LinkedIn is the usual place to reach a buyer by job title, company or a named account list. Meta can extend that reach, retarget people who already visited a page or saw an ad, and carry creative that would feel cramped in a professional feed. Google, YouTube, Reddit and other channels appear on this list too, but the LinkedIn plus Meta pairing is the one most teams ask about first.

A combined motion is more than two logins. It means one brief, a budget that can move when one channel stalls, and reporting that does not treat the two as separate stories. Several firms on this list name both LinkedIn and Meta on their own sites and run them under one plan. Others stay on LinkedIn only, which is a clean fit when that is the only paid social channel you want.

Ask also whether audiences can be shared in a way the platforms allow: for example, whether people who engaged with a LinkedIn ad can be reached again on Meta, and whether a CRM list can be used on both. If the firm cannot explain that handoff, the channels will be run in parallel even if they appear in the same proposal.

A combined plan is not a claim that two channels always outperform one. Ask who writes the creative for each, how budget moves between them and whether a lead from Meta is followed up the same way as a lead from LinkedIn. If the answers differ by channel, you are hiring two motions, not one.

Budget and stage fit

Fit depends on the stage of the company and on how the work is scoped, not on a generic spend chart. Some firms on this list describe a later-stage brief. Refine Labs, on its own site, says it works with B2B technology companies at Series B and beyond. AdConversion describes a fit with B2B SaaS companies from Series A through Series C. Omni Lab describes work with B2B SaaS teams and, on its homepage, lists a Series A to C example of a good fit. Be Leaded describes work with B2B companies and local businesses that want ads, a landing page and a fast reply.

Fee shape also changes with scope. This list includes monthly program fees, flat monthly management priced to the work rather than a percent of spend, management fees based on ad spend, custom engagements and monthly management with a multi-month minimum. We do not reprint dollar figures, spend minimums or competitor rate cards here. Ask each firm how the fee and the included work change as the program grows, and whether ad spend is paid to the platforms separately.

Creative scope is part of fit as well. Some firms on this list include in-house ad creative and landing pages in the paid program. Others scope creative as a separate line, or expect you to supply assets. Ask what is included before you compare fee shapes, because a lower management fee can hide a second invoice for design.

Stage language on a firm's site is a starting filter, not a grade. A Series B demand program is a different job from a local service campaign. Match the firm's stated audience to your brief, then confirm the model, the channels and the handoff on a call.

What you should compare

Channels and the job

LinkedIn is often the main B2B paid social channel, but Meta, Google, YouTube, Reddit and others appear on this list too. Match the channel mix to the buyer, not to a default package. If your buyers are reachable by job title or company name, LinkedIn is usually the first channel to test. If you also need to reach people who already visited a page, or you want a wider net around the same offer, Meta is often the second channel to consider. Google search is not paid social, but several firms on this list run it in the same program, which can matter when a buyer leaves the feed and starts looking by name.

Fee structure, not published prices

The engagements described here include monthly program fees, flat monthly management priced to the work rather than a percent of spend, management fees based on ad spend, custom engagements and monthly management with a multi-month minimum. Compare the shape and what is included. A flat fee can be easier to forecast as spend grows. A fee based on ad spend can rise when you scale a channel that is working. A multi-month minimum is a term, not a promise that the work will be ready in that window. We do not reprint competitor fee tables or dollar figures here, and this page does not list Be Leaded's price.

Creative, pages and follow-up

Ask who writes and designs the ads, who builds the landing page and what happens after a lead comes in. Some firms stop at the click. Others attach creative, pages or a reply in minutes. If follow-up is included, ask in whose name the message is sent, whether consent is captured on the form and whether a missed lead is tried again by email, text or a call. If creative is a separate line, ask how many new ads you should expect in a normal month and who approves them before they start running.

How results are measured

Ask whether reporting stops at cost per lead, or whether it follows pipeline, qualified opportunities or booked meetings. The right measure depends on the job you hired them for. If the job is pipeline, agree which CRM stage counts and how a paid social lead is marked when it becomes an opportunity. If the job is a booked meeting, agree what a qualified conversation means and who has to attend. A dashboard of clicks is not enough for either job, and a promised conversion rate is not a substitute for that agreement.

In house or hired help

Decide whether you need someone to run the channels every week, or only a second set of eyes on an account you already operate. The sections above on when to hire and on budget and stage fit are the place to make that call before you shortlist names. A hired team is not a replacement for a person inside your company who can approve creative, take a qualified conversation and say when a lead is not a fit. Without that counterpart, even a well-run program will stall after the click, and the report will not have a next step worth acting on.

One channel or a combined plan

If LinkedIn is the only paid social channel you want, a LinkedIn-only firm can be the cleaner fit. If you want LinkedIn and Meta under one brief, ask how budget, creative and reporting are shared. Do not assume a multi-channel logo means the two channels are actually run as one motion.

Who owns the account, the creative and the handoff

Ask who holds admin access to the ad accounts, who keeps the creative files if the work ends and what happens in the first minutes after a form is submitted. Some firms stop at the click. Others build the page and reply to the lead. The right handoff depends on whether your sales team can take that conversation.

How to shortlist and verify a firm

Start with the job, not with a logo. Decide whether you need a LinkedIn-only firm, a multi-channel paid social team or a program that includes the landing page and a reply to each lead. Then use the comparison table to pick a short list whose stated channels and fee shapes could actually do that job. Three names is usually enough. A longer list rarely helps if the brief is still vague.

Open the source links in each profile and read the firm's own pages, not only this guide. Confirm that the channel list, the fee shape and the audience they say they serve still match what you need. If a sentence here no longer appears on their site, treat their site as the record and ask them to explain the change on a call. Profile facts on this page were checked on November 12, 2026 and can go stale.

On that call, ask who will do the weekly work, how often you will speak, what the report contains and what happens when a lead arrives. Ask whether reporting can follow an opportunity or a booked meeting in your CRM, not only a platform conversion. Ask who owns the ad accounts, the pixel or the conversion setup, and the creative files if the work ends. If those answers are not clear, keep looking.

Be wary of a pitch that leans on a single case study, a promised number of meetings or a launch clock. This guide does not reprint those claims, and you should not have to accept them to hire a firm. It is fair to ask how they would start, what they need from your team and how you would stop at the end of a month or at the end of a stated term. It is also fair to walk away if the model on their site and the model in the sales conversation are not the same.

You are ready to hire when you have an offer that can be explained in an ad, a way to take the conversation after a lead arrives and a way to see what happened after the click. If those pieces are missing, an agency will still be able to spend, but it will not be able to finish the job this page is written around.

Questions

What does a B2B paid social agency do?

It plans and runs paid ads on social platforms, usually LinkedIn and Meta, to reach business buyers. Some agencies also run Google, YouTube or Reddit, and some add creative, landing pages or follow-up so a lead can become a meeting.

It is alphabetical only. A to Z placement is not a scoreboard, and no company is given stars or numeric grades. Use the fee model, channel and fit columns to build a short list, then confirm each claim on that firm's own website before you hire.

How does this guide differ from the Be Leaded paid social service page?

The service page is the place to hire Be Leaded for Meta, Google and LinkedIn campaigns with a reply to each enquiry. This guide compares several agencies, including Be Leaded, and states that we published it. If you already know you want us, use the service page.

What should I compare before hiring a firm?

Compare channels, fee shape (not just a headline price), who makes the creative and landing pages, what happens after a lead arrives and whether reporting follows pipeline or booked meetings rather than clicks alone. Also compare whether LinkedIn and Meta would run as one plan, and whether the firm's stated stage or audience matches your brief.

Do pharma or nonprofit teams use the same agencies?

Not always. Pharmaceutical campaigns have medical, legal and regulatory review, and platform rules for health content. Nonprofit fundraising has donation-page and cause-ad rules. Be Leaded keeps those jobs on separate scoping pages rather than on this general B2B list.

It is the option that pairs ads with a page and a reply: Meta, Google and LinkedIn campaigns, a landing page, a form and an answer by email, SMS or voice. We published this guide and appear as one agency on it. A to Z order is not a score.

When should we hire an agency instead of running ads in house?

Hire help when the weekly work, the creative testing and the reporting to pipeline or meetings are more than your team can cover. Keep the work in house when someone already runs the accounts well and can take each lead forward. This page does not promise that hiring beats an internal team.

Can LinkedIn and Meta run as one program?

Yes, when one brief, one budget plan and one report cover both. Several firms on this list name LinkedIn and Meta on their own sites.
