Visbl alternatives for search fund deal sourcing

By Be Leaded. Updated November 13, 2026.

Visbl on its own pages

What follows is a baseline taken only from Visbl. Placement on this guide is not a score, and no firm bought its mention. The Visbl homepage, the search fund deal sourcing page and the deal origination page were opened on November 12, 2026.

The search fund page describes writing to owners who match a stated thesis, from dedicated sending domains that are warmed before anything is sent, with the firm's own domain never used. Interested owners are sent to the inbox. The homepage says cold email is the engine, and LinkedIn outreach can run alongside it. A first month is described as build and warmup with no live sending, and outreach is described as starting in the following month.

Commercial terms on the search fund page are a flat monthly subscription sized to scope, with no success fees, no exclusivity and no per-lead charges. The same page states a refund of campaign fees if no qualified owner replies arrive in the first 90 days of live sending. Replies are read before they are passed on, and only owners inside the buy box who answered with intent are shown. The page also says the work uses a dedicated domain and runs alongside existing intermediary relationships.

Eight firm notes

The list has eight names. Be Leaded sits in the fifth slot only to keep it off the top of the page.

Axia Growth

Here because the public site names search funds as a buyer it serves, and it reaches owners by email and phone under an exclusive buyer campaign.

The Axia Growth homepage and the Axia Growth about page, read on November 12, 2026, describe M&A deal sourcing that introduces business owners to buyers whose criteria they fit. Buyers named on those pages include search funds, private equity firms, family offices, holdcos and strategic acquirers. Outreach is described as email and phone. Buyer campaigns are described as exclusive. Owners pay nothing. Owners who would consider selling get a screening call before an introduction is made.

Coseek

Here because the firm publishes a search fund page built around calling owners, and the homepage states a month to month term.

The Coseek search fund page, read on November 12, 2026, says Coseek researches the market, calls owners and books qualified meetings, while the searcher takes the owner conversations. The Coseek homepage describes a month of calling, then a decision to continue, change or stop. The term is month to month. Preparation is at Coseek's cost, and billing starts on the first day of calling. A fixed monthly fee is mentioned without a figure on that page.

Caprae Capital

Here because Search as a Service names four channels, including handwritten mail, and the about page states there are no long-term contracts.

The Search as a Service page, read on November 12, 2026, describes building target lists from a buy box and reaching owners by phone, email, LinkedIn and handwritten mail, then passing qualified owner conversations to the buyer. The page is written for traditional and self-funded searchers, independent sponsors and private equity firms. A success fee is named as part of the commercial model, without a figure repeated here. The Caprae Capital about page states: No long-term contracts, no lock-in.

OutSearched

Here because the public site describes finding, contacting and qualifying off-market owners by email, phone and direct mail, then staying with the process through NDAs and financials.

The OutSearched site, read on November 12, 2026, is the page that loaded for this check. The apex domain returned an access error from this build environment, so no fact is taken from a page that did not load. The live page describes defining a mandate, building a custom owner list, reaching owners by email, phone and direct mail, taking the first calls, getting NDAs signed and pulling financials. Weekly reports and a strategy call are included. Audiences named on that page are private equity groups, family offices, independent sponsors and serious individual buyers. Search funds are not named on the page that loaded, so they are not added here.

Be Leaded

Fifth in this list. Today's published offer is written for advisors and brokers on the sell side rather than as a dedicated searcher product.

The off-market deal flow page, read on November 12, 2026, says Be Leaded researches owners of private companies who might consider a sale, a recap or a valuation talk, then opens those conversations confidentially under the advisor's or broker's name. The firm on whose behalf we write gets the meetings. We do not advise on transactions. The published program covers email together with LinkedIn. Phone work is scoped only if you ask for it. The month to month lead generation page is the term sheet: No 3-month commitment, pay month by month. A program can be stopped when the current month ends. The contact file and every reply thread stay yours. Outbound mail is not sent from the company's primary domain. A related buy-side track for brokers with active listings is described on buyer outreach for business brokers.

Deal Prospectors

Here because the custom sourcing page names LinkedIn, email and paid social as a mix, and it states a cancellation point after 60 days.

The custom deal sourcing page, read on November 12, 2026, describes tailored campaigns that contact owners who match a target profile. Channels named are LinkedIn outreach, cold email, Google Ads and Meta Ads. Owners are qualified on size, reason for selling, timing, geography and headcount before a handoff. Handoff choices include a time booked on the calendar, a pre-screen by phone or contact details sent for the buyer to call. Audiences named include independent sponsors, self-funded searchers, private equity teams and family offices. A success fee is part of the published model, without a figure repeated here. The same page states: Cancel anytime after 60 days.

BD Flow

Here because the homepage describes managed, owner-direct origination and states there are no long-term lock-ins.

The BD Flow homepage, read on November 12, 2026, describes identifying targets, running proprietary outreach and delivering qualified owner conversations. Outreach is described as owner-direct and multichannel, without a named channel list on that page. Interested owners land in the inbox, and the buyer takes the call. Audiences named are PE-backed platforms, independent sponsors, family offices and holding companies. Search funds are not named on that homepage, so they are not added here. Engagements are described as flexible, with no long-term lock-ins.

Acquisition Atlas

Here because the firm publishes more than one commercial model, including a retained search and a success-fee introduction with no retainer.

The Acquisition Atlas homepage and the Acquisition Atlas about page, read on November 12, 2026, describe deal origination for private equity firms, independent sponsors and family offices. Retained search is a managed mandate: owners are reached, fit is qualified and conversations are delivered to the calendar, with research attached. A separate line, success-fee introductions, has no retainer, and the fee is earned only when a transaction closes. Search funds are not named as a served audience on the pages checked, so they are not added here.

How a conversation is handed over

A firm is mentioned in this section only if its own page describes the handoff. Visbl says interested owners are sent to the inbox, after replies are read and checked against the buy box. Coseek books the owner meeting and hands over why the owner agreed to talk. Caprae Capital passes qualified owner conversations, with call notes and next steps. OutSearched takes the first calls, handles NDAs and financials, then reports weekly. Axia Growth holds a screening call before an introduction. Deal Prospectors offers a booked calendar time, a phone pre-screen or contact details for the buyer to call. BD Flow sends interested owners to the inbox, and the buyer takes the call. Acquisition Atlas delivers qualified conversations to the calendar with research attached.

Be Leaded's live deal flow page starts confidential conversations in the advisor's or broker's name and books meetings with owners who want to talk. The firm does not advise on the deal. How that program sits next to a broker practice is also described on the M&A advisors and business brokers page.

Records to take with you

Before notice runs out, ask for written answers to four points: who keeps the owner list, who keeps the email or call threads, who keeps the pipeline rows and who can still authenticate the sending domains. Put that request to Visbl and to every other name on this guide.

Visbl's search fund page says outreach uses a dedicated domain and that the firm's own domain is never used. Be Leaded's published program pages describe data ownership clearly. The contact file and every reply thread stay yours. Outbound mail is not sent from the company's primary domain. Exporting a spreadsheet is not the same as controlling the authentication records that make a sending domain yours.
