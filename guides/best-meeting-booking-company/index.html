The best meeting booking company for B2B is the one that books people who fit your ICP, puts a real time on the calendar, and gets them to show. This page names seven companies and the job each one is built for. It is not a paid ranking.

Outsourced meeting booking means another company finds the prospect, handles the reply, and puts the meeting on your calendar. Your team still takes the meeting and closes. What a qualified booked meeting should mean

Agree this in writing before you compare price. A qualified booked meeting has three parts. Miss one, and the number on a proposal is a different product.

  • ICP fit. Title, company size, industry, and the problem match the definition you agreed before launch. A meeting with the wrong person is not qualified.
  • Booked. A real time is on your calendar, with context about who they are and what they want to talk about. A positive reply that never becomes a time is not a booked meeting.
  • Attended. The person shows up. Ask who owns a no-show, and whether a reschedule still counts.

Be Leaded does not promise a meeting count before looking at the market. A yearly appointment guarantee is not the same thing as this definition. The contract still has to name title, company, agenda, and no-shows.

Belkins: best for packaged appointment volume

Best for packaged appointment volume, if you want a yearly appointment tier and will confirm a starter Belkins lists from $5,000.

Belkins sells appointment setting and monthly retainer packages. On October 1, 2026, the appointment setting page listed an average starter price from $5,000, with 1,500 leads a month, three outreach channels, and 100 guaranteed appointments a year (belkins.io/appointment-setting). Structured data on that same page lists a minimum price of $8,000, so the visible starter and the schema do not match. The pricing page describes retainers by yearly appointments: 30 or more for the small-business package, 100 or more for Growth, and 200 or more for Growth Plus, plus an Enterprise tier. It lists cold email, LinkedIn, calling, messenger, account-based marketing, and paid advertising, and it does not publish dollar prices (belkins.io/pricing). Confirm the quote with Belkins.

Ask what a guaranteed appointment means in the contract: title, company size, show rate, and who owns a no-show. A yearly tier is a volume product. It fits when your offer is already stable enough to absorb that many conversations. If that package is the wrong fit, the side-by-side is Belkins alternatives.

CIENCE: best for a GTM system plus SDR seats

Best for a GTM system plus optional SDR seats, with prices published by level and region.

CIENCE publishes a GTM setup plus optional SDR capacity. On October 1, 2026, the pricing page listed a one-time GTM System Setup at $5,000, strategic execution at $2,000 a month, and a graph8 platform license at $499 a month. It also listed a first month at $7,499 for setup plus team plus platform, with SDR capacity quoted separately (cience.com/pricing). SDR seats on that page: Level 1 at $1,500 offshore, $2,500 in Europe, or $4,500 in the US; Level 2 at $2,500, $3,500, or $5,500; Level 3 at $3,500, $4,500, or $6,500. A $1,000 one-time fee per SDR is listed there too. The homepage separately says managed SDR and campaigns from $5,600 a month (cience.com). The pricing page says SDR capacity can be added without a long-term contract. The SDR services terms say the initial term is twelve months unless the order form says otherwise (SDR services terms). Read both before you sign.

The useful question is which number you are buying. The homepage figure, the $7,499 first month, and the SDR seat table are three different lines on CIENCE's own site. Add them up before you compare CIENCE with a single meeting booking fee.

Martal Group: best for fractional sales that include discovery

Best for an onshore fractional sales team that can run discovery and, on higher tiers, closing.

Martal Group publishes three tiers and does not publish a dollar retainer. Checked October 1, 2026 (martal.ca/pricing). Tier 1 outbound lead generation is a flat fee per month, with a 3 month pilot, then a monthly subscription. The scope on that page is email, LinkedIn, and calls, with appointment booking and discovery calls led by a North American team. Tier 2 adds sales and customer onboarding. Tier 3 adds account management. Tiers 2 and 3 are a flat fee per month plus a sales commission, with a 4 month pilot. Clutch's appointment setting rankings, checked the same day, list Martal Group at $25 to $49 an hour and a minimum project size of $1,000+ (Clutch). That is a directory band, not a quote. Ask Martal for the current fee.

If you only need a qualified meeting on a calendar, Tier 2 and Tier 3 are more process than that. The pilot length is the commercial fact: three months on Tier 1 outbound, four months when they also sell and onboard.

Leadium: best for a month-to-month published retainer

Best for month-to-month US outbound when you want a published retainer and no appointment-count guarantee.

Leadium's pricing page, checked October 1, 2026, says contracts are month to month, that appointment setting typically launches in 7 to 10 days, and that Leadium does not guarantee appointment outcomes (leadium.com/pricing). That page does not list a dollar price. Leadium's own outbound pricing guide lists $3,500 a month for cold calling only and $4,000 to $5,000 a month for multi-channel outbound across phone, email, and LinkedIn, month to month (Leadium outbound pricing guide). The pricing page also describes a US-based team and lists a Las Vegas address.

Leadium is explicit that appointment counts are not guaranteed, and that clients approve the work. That is a cleaner contract than a guarantee with a soft definition of qualified. The dollar figures live on their guide, not on the pricing page, so attach the guide to the proposal you sign.

Cleverly: best for LinkedIn-first plans from $397 a month

Best for LinkedIn-first outreach on a published plan from $397 a month.

Cleverly's FAQ, checked October 1, 2026, says lead generation plans range from $397 a month to $997 a month. The same FAQ points cold email to a pay-per-meeting-ready-lead model and does not list a dollar price for it (cleverly.co/faq). The LinkedIn pricing page shows three plans at $397, $697, and $997 a month (Cleverly LinkedIn pricing). Treat the published dollars as LinkedIn plans unless a call prices email.

Cleverly is the shortlist pick when the test is LinkedIn and the buyer will live inside one profile's weekly limits. It is a weak pick when the meeting has to start in the inbox, because the published prices on the pages above are LinkedIn plans.

SalesRoads: best for a dedicated sales floor

Best for a dedicated outsourced sales floor, billed every four weeks, with calling in the base program.

SalesRoads' pricing page, checked October 1, 2026, lists one SDR at $11,950 per four weeks, two SDRs at $16,750 per four weeks, and three SDRs at $23,500 per four weeks (salesroads.com/pricing). The same page says the program includes calling, personalized email, and email infrastructure on multiple domains and mailboxes. It says you can cancel at any time, and that a team launches in 15 business days, with calling on the 16th business day.

SalesRoads prices a team, not a meeting. One SDR at $11,950 per four weeks is a different product from a $2,000 monthly program. It fits when phone is the channel and you want coaches, research, and a dashboard in the same fee.

FAQ

What is the best meeting booking company in 2026?

There is no single best. Be Leaded fits high-personalization outbound and qualified meetings from $2,000 a month. Belkins fits packaged appointment volume. CIENCE fits a GTM system plus SDR seats. Martal Group fits a fractional team that can run discovery. Leadium fits month-to-month retainers. Cleverly fits LinkedIn-first plans from $397 a month. SalesRoads fits a dedicated floor from $11,950 per four weeks.

What is the best meeting booking service for B2B?

For B2B, pick the service whose meeting definition matches the buyer you can sell to. Be Leaded fits when you want email and LinkedIn, replies handled the same business day, and programs from $2,000 a month. Belkins fits when you want a yearly appointment package and will confirm the starter they list from $5,000.

What does outsourced meeting booking include?

It should include the list, the sending domains, the copy, reply handling, and the booking. At Be Leaded, cold email goes out on separate sending domains, you approve sequences before they send, and most programs go live in about three weeks. A positive reply is not the product. A qualified meeting on your calendar is.

What should a qualified booked meeting mean?

Three things, in writing. ICP fit: the title, company, and problem match the definition you agreed before launch. Booked: a real time is on the calendar, with context. Attended: the person shows up, and the contract says who owns a no-show. Be Leaded does not promise a meeting count before looking at the market.

How much do meeting booking companies charge?
