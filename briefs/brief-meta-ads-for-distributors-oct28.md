### H2: What we run on Meta (`id="what-we-run"`)

Lead: Campaign types that fit distribution, using the Meta capabilities already on our paid social and search page.

- Dealer and reseller recruitment: Facebook and Instagram lead generation aimed at owners and buyers who could carry your line. Instant forms or a landing page, with the offer clear in one screen.
- B2B account acquisition: Campaigns that open wholesale accounts or book an intro with your outside sales team. Audiences split by intent, with creative refreshed before fatigue sets in.
- Retargeting: People who visited a product page, opened a form or engaged with a prior ad see a tighter offer. Built from your customer list and site visitors the same way we build retargeting on every Meta program.
- Lead forms into your CRM: Instant forms or landing pages with consent in plain view. Leads route into your CRM or inbox so your team, or our follow up, can act while the request is still warm.
- Follow up in minutes: SMS, email and AI voice follow up wired to every lead, plus a sequence for leads that go quiet. See [AI voice follow up](/ai-voice-agents/) for how the call back works.
- Lookalikes from your best accounts: Lookalike audiences built from your customer list so Meta finds firms that resemble the dealers and accounts you already win.

Close: The full platform list, including Google search and LinkedIn when they fit, lives on [paid social and search](/paid-social-agency/). This page stays on Meta for distributors and wholesalers.

### H2: Most Meta budgets leak after the click.

Lead: A dealer form that sits until morning is a lost account. We treat the click and the follow up as one job.

Compare rows (Stage / What usually happens / What we do):
- The ad: One creative, one audience, left running until the cost per lead doubles. / Several angles tested weekly, audiences split by intent, and creative refreshed before fatigue sets in.
- The form: A generic instant form with no consent language, so the leads cannot legally be texted or called. / A short form or landing page with consent captured in plain view, so email, SMS and voice follow up are all allowed.
- The follow up: Leads sit in a spreadsheet until someone has time. By then they have called another distributor. / A text within minutes, a call from a voice agent or your team, and an email sequence for anyone who goes quiet.

### H2: What you get

Lead: Ads, pages, forms and follow up from one team, so nothing falls between them.

- Meta campaign management: Facebook and Instagram campaigns for lead generation, with instant forms or landing pages, retargeting and lookalikes built from your customer list.
- Landing pages and forms: Fast pages built for one offer, with consent language that lets you follow up by text and voice.
- Follow up in minutes: SMS, email and AI voice follow up wired to every lead, plus a sequence for leads that go quiet.
- Reporting on meetings: Cost per lead is the start. We report cost per booked appointment and per customer, because those are the numbers that matter.
- Weekly testing: Angles, audiences and creative reviewed every week. What does not work gets cut. What works gets more budget.
- A path into demand gen: When you want Meta beside outbound and newsletters in one plan, move up to [demand generation](/demand-generation-agency/). For list and email led programs, see [B2B lead generation](/lead-generation-services/).

### FAQ

1. Q: What are Meta Ads for distributors?
   A: Meta Ads for distributors are Facebook and Instagram campaigns built for wholesale and distribution firms. Be Leaded runs lead generation with instant forms or landing pages, retargeting and lookalikes from your customer list, then follows up with every lead by email, text or voice within minutes.

2. Q: Which campaign types fit a distributor or wholesaler?
   A: Dealer and reseller recruitment, B2B account acquisition, retargeting to people who already visited your site or opened a form, and lead forms that feed your CRM and follow up stack. Each one uses the same Meta lead generation setup we run on our [paid social and search](/paid-social-agency/) page.

3. Q: Do you build the landing page and the form?
   A: Yes. We build a short form or landing page with consent captured in plain view, so email, SMS and voice follow up are all allowed. Instant forms on Meta are an option when the offer fits one screen.

4. Q: Do you do the follow up after the lead comes in?
   A: Yes. That is the point. Every lead gets a text within minutes, a call from a voice agent or your team, and an email sequence if they go quiet. Consent is captured on the form so all three channels are allowed.

5. Q: How soon do campaigns go live?
   A: From the first call to live campaigns in about two weeks. Week one is the landing page, forms, tracking, audiences, first creative and the follow up sequences. Week two is launch.

6. Q: How soon will we see leads?
   A: Leads usually arrive within days of launch. Cost per lead settles over the first few weeks as the platform learns and we cut what does not work.

7. Q: Can Meta sit next to outbound or LinkedIn?
   A: Yes. When you want Meta in the same plan as outbound and newsletters, aimed at booked calls, that is [demand generation](/demand-generation-agency/). [LinkedIn lead generation](/linkedin-lead-generation-agency/) is a separate track when job titles and company lists matter more than Meta reach.
